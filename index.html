THE ESSENTIALS

Three habits that improve security

🔑

Use unique passwords

Use a different password for every account. Reusing passwords can put multiple accounts at risk if one password is exposed.

🔐

Use a password manager

A reputable password manager can generate and securely store strong, unique passwords so you don't have to memorize each one.

🛡️

Enable MFA

Multifactor authentication adds another layer of protection by requiring an additional way to verify your identity.

Think before you trust.

Never share your password or verification codes with someone who asks for them unexpectedly. Be cautious of suspicious messages and login links.

Remember: Password security is one part of staying safe online. Keep your devices updated and secure your recovery options, too.
